Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Nebulyx is a studio run by Sonu Kumar in Chandigarh, India. This policy explains what information we collect through nebulyx.live, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect

  • Enquiry details you send us: your name, email address, the service you are interested in, your business stage, your preferred call time and any message you write.
  • Emails you send us at hello@nebulyx.live, including anything you attach.
  • Basic technical data that our hosting provider records when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, used to keep the site running and secure.

We do not use advertising trackers or sell your information.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and arrange a call.
  • To prepare a written scope, timeline and price if you ask for one.
  • To deliver and support any project you agree with us.

Services that handle your data

  • Vercel hosts this website.
  • Brevo delivers the enquiry emails sent from our form.
  • ImprovMX and Google route and store email sent to hello@nebulyx.live.
  • Google Fonts and Fontshare serve the typefaces used on this site.

These providers process data on our behalf and may store it outside India.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries and related emails for as long as needed to respond and, if we work together, for the length of the project and any period required for accounting or legal reasons. You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or ask us to stop contacting you. Email hello@nebulyx.live and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
